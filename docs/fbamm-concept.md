In my understanding, AMM like uniswap is successful way practical than the onchain LOB tried early years because its elegance and memory efficient is well suited to the blockchain. Only thing it needs to maintain is the two value (uniswap v2), stands for the current reserve level for a token pair. Each transaction update this pair of number, including swap, mint or burn. Price is determined by the ratio of this quantity pair. However, it brings the MEV problem and the liqudity efficiency issue. First, MEV comes from the intent before finialization. Each transaction is public to see before execute with its own price and tolerence level, so the MEV bot can frontrun this transcation up to its tolerence level. Second, even with 100 buy and 100 sell, there is no way to net out the two side orders. Both of them consume current liqudity. And if the liquidity is not enough, the price impact would hurt the buyer or sellers. So there is no clearing house idea in the current AMM. Now, pay attention, we introduce a FBA layer, on top of the AMM. Instead of only maintaining two numbers, we maintain 4 numbers to keep the memory-efficient feature. Two, as before are current reserve, and the extra two are the innovation, which is the Qb and Qs, stands for Buy Quantity, and Selling Quantity. When buyer or seller try to transact in our AMM, (lets call FBAMM), they cannot directly interact with the LP, the Qb will increase certain amount based on how much unit they want to buy, and same for the seller side. The Qb and Qs will accumulate until a clearing action being triggered by anyone, who will pay the gas fee but we can award him with the tansaction fee. and We limit the clearing events once per block. This will have two advantages. 1. MEV bot is no longer capable to frontrun the pending transaction because after the clearing, there will be a unified price for all. The unified price is determined by the gap btw Qb and Qs. For example, if we have all Qb and Qs matched, there is no price impact, and the unified price would be current AMM spot price. If we have more Qb than Qs, then (Qb-Qs) would consume the current liqudity, for example X0 and Y0 will be X0-(Qb-Qs) and Y0 also updated based on constant product. And unified price would be the post-swap AMM spot price for all traders in the Qb and Qs. MEV bot cannot front run and back run a transaction because of the one clearing per block limitation. It would be not determined profits if MEV bot cross different blocks, discentivize the bots. 2. The liqudity efficiency will be improved in the FBAMM, because with the clearing mechanism, the transactions will net with counterparty, and only the extra orders will be consuming the current liqudity.
